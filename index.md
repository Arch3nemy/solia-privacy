# Solia Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

Solia is a booking and shoot-day app for wedding photographers. This policy
explains what happens to information in the app.

## The short version

Everything you enter in Solia stays on your phone. There is no account and no
Solia server. The only information that leaves your device automatically is a
crash report when the app goes wrong, and a crash report contains nothing you
have typed.

## What stays on your device

Your jobs, your clients' names and contact details, venues, fees, invoices,
contracts, running orders, reference photos and your business details are
stored on your phone. We never receive them and cannot see them.

**Android's own backup.** If you have Android backup turned on, Android may copy
Solia's data — but not your reference photos — to your own Google account, so
it can be restored if you change or lose your phone. Solia only allows this
when Android can encrypt the backup end to end with your phone's screen lock, so
neither Google nor we can read it. If your phone has no screen lock, Solia is
left out of the backup. You control this in your phone's backup settings, and
you can delete the backup from your Google account at any time.

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone.

When you export a PDF or a backup file and share it — by email, messenger or
any other app — it goes where you send it. We do not receive a copy.

Solia asks for one permission: to show notifications. Photos are added through
your phone's system photo picker, so Solia only ever gets the photos you choose,
never access to your photo library.

## What we collect: crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to our crash
reporting provider so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

- technical details of the error (the stack trace)
- the app version
- your device model and operating system version
- when an app session started and ended
- a random identifier generated by the app on your device, used only to count
  how many installations an error affects

A crash report does **not** contain your clients' details or anything else you
entered, screenshots, your name, your email address, or an advertising
identifier. We have configured our provider not to store IP addresses.

**Why:** to keep the app working. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in
finding and fixing faults (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Who processes it:** Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), with data stored in
its EU region in Frankfurt, Germany.

**How long:** crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment under
its own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details — only whether a
subscription is active.

## Your clients' data

If you use Solia to keep details about your own clients, you are responsible
for that data under data protection law. Solia keeps it on your device and does
not share it with us or anyone else.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete
personal data about you, and to object to its processing. Because crash reports
are not linked to your name or account, we usually cannot tell which reports
came from you — but write to us and we will help as far as we can. You also have
the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

Solia is a tool for professionals and is not directed at children under 16.
